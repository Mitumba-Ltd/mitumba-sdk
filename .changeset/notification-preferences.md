`sdk.notifications` can read and change notification preferences: `getPreferences`, `updatePreferences` (category × channel cells, `null` to reset), `updatePreferenceSettings` (quiet hours and muted pop-ups) and `setMarketingConsent`. New types: `NotificationPreferences`, `NotificationPreferenceCategory`, `NotificationChannel`, `QuietHours`, `MutableFlashKind`, `PreferenceCellChange`, `PreferenceSettingsChange` and `MarketingConsentChange`.
